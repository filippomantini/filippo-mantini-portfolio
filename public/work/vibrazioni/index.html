VIBRAZIONI

Vibrazioni is an experimental sound-printing installation in which music becomes a physical graphic trace.

SOUND BECOMES MOVEMENT. MOVEMENT BECOMES A MARK.
01 / CONTEXT

FROM SOUND
TO SURFACE.

The project starts from a simple question: what happens when sound is not only heard, but allowed to leave a physical trace?

Guitar strings carrying ink react to music and vibration, transferring variations in frequency, amplitude and timbre onto paper.

02 / SYSTEM

A DEVICE AS
A DRAWING TOOL.

The installation is designed as a system in which audio input, vibration, material and chance work together.

Each output is unique. The visual result is not illustrated after the sound; it is produced by the sound itself through a physical process.

03 / OUTPUT

AN IMAGE THAT
CAN'T REPEAT.

The printed traces become a record of a specific sonic moment and of the behaviour of the device.

Presented at CasermArcheologica, the project sits between graphic design, sound, installation and performance.
