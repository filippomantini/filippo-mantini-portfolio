A proposed visual identity for Città di Castello, built around the city's cultural relationship with Alberto Burri. The project translates forms found in his work into a civic mark, a custom alphabet and a flexible visual language.

01 / CONTEXT

A CITY, ONE
VISUAL VOICE.

The project began with a simple question: how can a city be translated into a recognisable visual identity? The aim was to create a shared system capable of connecting institutions, culture and public communication through one coherent language.

Rather than designing a single logo, the proposal develops a visual system that can adapt to different areas of city communication while maintaining continuity and recognition.

02 / CONCEPT

FROM BURRI
TO THE CITY.

Alberto Burri became the visual starting point of the identity. Selected shapes were isolated from his artworks, simplified and transformed into letterforms.

From this process emerged the CDC symbol and a custom typographic vocabulary rooted in the artistic heritage of Città di Castello.

04 / APPLICATIONS

FROM INSTITUTION
TO CITY.

The system was developed across institutional and public-facing applications, including stationery, press communication, posters, office signage, wayfinding and merchandise.

Each application follows the same hierarchy while allowing the identity to adapt to different scales and functions.
