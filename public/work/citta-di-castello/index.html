01 / CONTEXT

IDENTITY AS
A SYSTEM.

The project approaches the city as a living visual environment. The identity is designed to work across different contexts while maintaining a recognisable rhythm and structure.

Instead of relying on a single application, the system is built to expand through typography, composition and repeatable graphic rules.
