COLLAPSE / EVENTVOL IV / 2025

IVORY

One night, one visual language. The communication and production of COLLAPSE PARTY VOL IV, with IVORY at Formula Disco.

19.12.2025Formula Disco / Jeko Room
ProjectCOLLAPSE / IVORY
My roleCreative direction · Graphic design · Photography · Event organisation and production
01 / CAMPAIGN

Identity and motion

Black and white, photographic grain and a restrained typographic system connect the guest announcement, the promotional reel and the event information.

The portrait of IVORY becomes the centre of a visual language that moves from the Instagram feed to stories and the venue entrance.

02 / SOCIAL SYSTEM

Before the night

The same visual system structures practical information: ticket releases, set times and the reserved entrance route. A coordinated series accompanies the audience from the announcement to the final tickets.

03 / IN THE SPACE

From screen to venue

The exterior banner extends the campaign into the physical space. The COLLAPSE mark, IVORY portrait and directional arrow identify the entrance to the private party.

04 / THE NIGHT

Inside the event

The video recap brings the project back to the dance floor: the audience, the music and the atmosphere of the night.
