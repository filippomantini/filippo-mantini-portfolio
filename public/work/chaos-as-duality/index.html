01 / PRIVATE PARTY

CHAOS AS DUALITY.

20 December 2024, Mojito Club, Città di Castello. A private COLLAPSE event preceded by an aperitivo at La Piazzetta. My work covers both the visual identity and the organisation of the night.

02 / EVENT MANAGEMENT

MY ROLE,
FROM PLANNING TO THE NIGHT.

For the party I handled PR, ticket management, publishing, setup and physical materials. Communication and event organisation were developed together.

  • PR and audienceEvent promotion and contact with invited guests.
  • Tickets and admissionTicket management and event admission materials.
  • Production and setupCoordination of the setup, publications and wristbands.
03 / PRE-PARTY

THE NIGHT BEGINS
AT LA PIAZZETTA.

I handled the pre-party at Bar La Piazzetta, from drink selection to identity and publications. Underdog, Blizzard, Awareness and Overturn form the dedicated menu; its graphics introduce the visual language of the party.
