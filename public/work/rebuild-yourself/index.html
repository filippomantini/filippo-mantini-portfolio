REBUILDYOURSELF.

Art is never neutral. Every collapse is an opening. Every fracture is a passage.

PROJECTA multidisciplinary exhibition, private party and visual system built around reuse, rupture and transformation.
ROLEArt Direction
Graphic Design / Editorial
01 / MANIFESTOCOLLAPSE IS A PROCESS

DON’T REBUILD.
REINVENT.

Our task is not to rebuild, but to reinvent: in new, unpredictable and necessary forms. Collapse is a process, an energy in continuous transformation that tears down and reassembles, recovers and recreates.

Creativity cannot be divided into fixed categories, styles or schools: it is made of visions and transformations. Every encounter, dialogue and insight becomes part of a living, moving mosaic.

02 / VISUAL SYSTEM

AN IDENTITY
IN THE MAKING.

Rebuild Yourself brings contemporary art, fashion and music together as a cultural project in the heart of Città di Castello. Tools of making become a graphic language of handmade marks, disruption and overlap.

Original drawings — chains, nails, buttons, stitches and working tools — move freely across printed matter and page layouts. A distinctive, but never rigid, visual grammar.

03 / THE A3 FOLDOUT

ONE A3.
MANY READINGS.

The A3 folded sheet was designed as a layered editorial object: each fold reveals different information. Only when the sheet is fully unfolded do its parts come together as the event's complete poster.

The A3 was not a traditional flyer: its folded surfaces revealed information progressively, while the fully opened front became a single poster. Shown here are the original print artwork, reverse and folding study.

04 / HAND-DRAWN LANGUAGETOOLS / MATERIALS / TRANSFORMATION

MARKS OF MAKING.
MARKS OF CHANGE.

Hand-drawn symbols became the visual foundation of the exhibition's communications. Tools of making, sewing, reuse and transformation formed a graphic language carried across the poster, folded leaflet, printed displays, invitations and digital campaign.

05 / EXHIBITION TEXTS

EXHIBITION
AS ARCHIVE.

Eight exhibition captions, mounted on OSB boards, told the project's creative stages through titles and texts. Restrained typography set against raw material was part of the exhibition's identity.
