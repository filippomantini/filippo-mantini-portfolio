BREAK. REASSEMBLE. REBUILD YOURSELF.
01 / CONTEXT

ONE SPACE,
MULTIPLE LANGUAGES.

Rebuild Yourself brought together contemporary art, fashion and music inside the Loggiato di Palazzo Bufalini in Città di Castello.

The project required a visual language capable of connecting exhibition, event communication and the broader Collapse identity without reducing them to the same thing.

02 / SYSTEM

AN EVENT AS
A VISUAL WORLD.

Art direction, printed matter, spatial communication and digital promotion were developed as a single experience.

The visual system was designed to support both the exhibition and the private party, changing intensity while remaining recognisable.

03 / OUTPUT

FROM INVITATION
TO EXPERIENCE.

The project moved from communication into physical space, where audience, work, clothing and sound became part of the final identity.

It represents Collapse as a cultural platform as much as a fashion project.
