COLLAPSE × BAR LA PIAZZETTA2024 / PROJECT

BAR LA
PIAZZETTA

I developed the identity for COLLAPSE aperitivi at La Piazzetta, selected drinks and handled communication: menus, posters, social assets and the event giveaway. The collaboration continued with the Chaos as Duality pre-party.

Role
Identity / Drink selection / Communication
Location
Città di Castello, IT
Period
June → December 2024
01 / COLLASSO BLP

AN APERITIVO,
AN IDENTITY.

On 7 June 2024, Collasso BLP brings COLLAPSE to Bar La Piazzetta. A black-and-white checkerboard connects the brand with the venue across posters, menus, social publications and giveaways.

02 / DRINK & SOCIAL

FROM THE MENU
TO PUBLICATION.

I selected the drinks and designed their graphic presentation. Each drink name becomes a social publication within the same visual system as the menu and printed materials.

03 / GADGET

AN IDENTITY
TO TAKE WITH YOU.

The aperitivo giveaway is a lanyard keychain: the checkerboard and COLLAPSE lettering move from communication onto a wearable object.

04 / SPAZIO

GRAPHICS
MEET THE VENUE.

The project also includes proposals for facade posters and the DJ booth wrap. These are the original project presentation boards.

05 / CHAOS AS DUALITY

THE PRE-PARTY
AT LA PIAZZETTA.

In December the collaboration continues with the Chaos as Duality aperitivo. Black and white gives way to acid green: I handled the identity, drink selection and pre-party, connecting it with the night at Mojito Club.
