01 / CONTEXT

A MORE
CONTEMPORARY IDENTITY.

The project began with the need to evolve the previous mark into an identity that could feel current without losing clarity or trust. The system was conceived to work consistently across corporate communication, print and web.

The redesign focuses on recognition and versatility: one visual language able to move between institutional material and a more expressive digital environment.

02 / BRANDMARK

A + P
+ PLUS.

The new brandmark geometrically combines A, P and the “+” symbol into one autonomous sign. Rather than treating the plus as an accessory, it becomes part of the structure of the mark and the wider visual language.

The result can work both as a standalone symbol and together with the Azienda Più / Broker Lalli lockup.

03 / VISUAL SYSTEM

FOUR COLORS.
ONE VOICE.

A new typographic hierarchy and a four-colour palette extend the mark into a repeatable system. Each colour has a defined role across institutional, digital and printed communication.

Royal Blue carries accents and calls to action; Deep Navy holds institutional communication; Midnight defines the darker digital environment; Warm White gives print and interface space to breathe.

04 / DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

IDENTITY BECOMES
INTERFACE.

The website treats the identity as interface. Large typography, deep blue fields, bright call-to-action accents and responsive navigation keep the system recognisable while adapting to different screen sizes.

The content architecture makes the broker's method visible: understanding the company, analysing risk, exploring the market, building a solution and staying alongside the client over time.

Not a policy to sell.
A solution built around the company.

05 / APPLICATIONS

FROM SCREEN
TO CONTACT.

The same identity moves into stationery, business cards and contact applications, keeping the relationship between blue, white space and the AP+ mark intact.

The objective is not to create separate visual moments, but to let the same system change scale without changing voice.
