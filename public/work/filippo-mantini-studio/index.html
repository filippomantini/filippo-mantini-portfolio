01 / Starting point

Organising the work around the project.

Creative projects also involve requests, files, deadlines and decisions. Studio brings these activities into a personal environment, keeping the work that needs attention visible.

The project connects graphic design with digital tools: typographic hierarchy, navigation and workflows are built around daily activities, from email to preparing a video.

02 / Product logic

Understand first.
Then decide.

The interface separates reading information from actions that change it. Review is a distinct step in the workflow, with visible states and confirmations.

  1. 01

    Read

    Bring requests, open tasks and results together in the Today view. Dedicated sections provide the details.

  2. 02

    Organise

    Sort email, assign tasks to projects and prepare briefs, scripts and content production sheets.

  3. 03

    Approve

    Check proposals and results before taking action. Changes to email follow an explicit confirmation process.

03 / Visual language

An interface designed to be read.

Simple typography, indices and fine rules organise the information. The same structure runs through the different sections, so changing tasks does not require learning a new interface.

Application typography / Helvetica, ArialAa 01

Headings, lists and states. An editorial hierarchy applied to an operational tool.

Light and dark surfaces, high-contrast text and an accent for items that need review.

One structure, several sections

Sidebar navigation, a central content area and readable states. On mobile, the structure becomes a single column.

Google services and data use

With user consent, Filippo Mantini Studio can connect to Gmail to read, classify and manage email. Actions that modify messages require explicit confirmation. Data access and revoking authorisation are explained in the Studio privacy policy.
