A creative operating system designed to organize everyday work in a multidisciplinary studio. One space for projects, email, video production and operations.

01 / The project

One workspace.
Many disciplines.

Filippo Mantini Studio is a private application running locally on a computer. It brings together tools for tasks, projects, and creative workflows, with third-party integrations authorized by the user.

02 / Approach

Designing
work itself.

A project combining art direction, interface design and digital product development — from visual language to information architecture and operational tools.

03 / Integrations

Personal
control.

With the user's permission, the application may connect to Google services such as Gmail to read, classify and manage messages. Actions that modify messages, such as moving them to Trash, require explicit user approval.

This page presents the project; the locally installed software is not publicly accessible.
