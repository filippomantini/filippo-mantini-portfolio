AXION
CONTROLLERS

I worked on Axion’s identity and digital experience: from the mark to product presentation, from the e-commerce website to the controller configuration journey.

Role
Visual identity / Web design / Digital product
Scope
Logo system / E-commerce / 3D / Product image
Project
Axion Controllers
01 / BRAND IDENTITY

AN IDENTITY
FOR CONTROL.

Axion builds custom controllers. The identity needs to identify the brand on a small product and guide technical choices online: the logo, hierarchy, colour and imagery work together.

02 / LOGO SYSTEM

READING
THE STRUCTURE OF THE MARK.

The symbol combines four diagonal arms and four directional elements around an open centre. It evokes intersecting axes and controller inputs. The boards isolate parts of the original vector artwork to show how they come together.

01 / Diagonal structure

02 / Four directions

03 / The complete mark

Space, contrast, reduction.

The proposed usage rule reserves at least one module x around the mark, where x is one eighth of the symbol width. The mark retains the same construction in positive and reversed monochrome versions.

Reduction study / 64, 40, 24 px

03 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

COLOUR
AS A SIGNAL.

Deep navy supports the imagery and interface; lime identifies actions and selections, while off-white keeps text and data readable. On the website, the Inter sans-serif family organises headings, descriptions and technical information.

04 / DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

FROM THE PRODUCT
TO THE USER’S CHOICES.

The configurator divides the choice into seven steps: rear inputs, Smart inputs, sticks, shell, customisation, production and review. The interface displays selections and price, then packages the configuration into a shareable code.

05 / PRODUCT IMAGE

FORM, GRIP,
DETAIL.

Product images show the whole controller and the areas affected by customisation: rear inputs, grip, triggers and finishes. The sequence alternates general views with close-up details.

06 / MOVING IMAGE

THE PRODUCT
IN MOTION.

Four short visual studies present the rear, materials, gaming station and TMR module. Movement changes the viewpoint and reveals product features.
