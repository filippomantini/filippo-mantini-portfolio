FM—004 / AXION CONTROLLERS2025—2026 / IDENTITY & DIGITAL

AXION
CONTROLLERS

For Axion Controllers I worked on the website, visual identity, all photography and image editing. The work also includes Instagram publications and Twitch graphics for players.

Role
Web design / Visual identity / Photography
Scope
E-commerce / Photo editing / Instagram / Twitch
Project
Axion Controllers

Project in brief

Objective
Develop the visual presence of Axion, an existing custom-controller brand, through its website, product imagery and communication for Instagram and Twitch.
Design constraint
The photographs need to show both the whole object and small components, such as rear buttons and triggers. The same material must adapt to web pages, vertical posts and banners.
Decision
I paired front and rear views with detail photographs. Each MH4 publication focuses on one component, while the banners bring together product variants.
Delivered work
I produced the website, photography and editing, Instagram publications and player graphics. This page presents the original materials and identifies the Twitch mockup as a demonstration.
01 / BRAND IDENTITY

AN IDENTITY
FOR CONTROL.

Axion builds custom controllers. The identity needs to identify the brand on a small product and guide technical choices online: the logo, hierarchy, colour and imagery work together.

02 / LOGO SYSTEM

STRUCTURE
OF THE SYMBOL.

The symbol combines four diagonal arms and four directional elements around an open centre. It evokes intersecting axes and controller inputs. The boards isolate parts of the original vector artwork to show how they come together.

01 / Diagonal structure

02 / Four directions

03 / The complete mark

Space, contrast, reduction.

The proposed usage rule reserves at least one module x around the mark, where x is one eighth of the symbol width. The mark retains the same construction in positive and reversed monochrome versions.

Reduction study / 64, 40, 24 px

03 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

COLOUR
AS A SIGNAL.

Deep navy supports the imagery and interface; lime identifies actions and selections, while off-white keeps text and data readable. On the website, the Inter sans-serif family organises headings, descriptions and technical information.

04 / DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

STRUCTURE, IMAGES,
NAVIGATION.

I worked on the website as the meeting point of visual identity, photography and product information. The work covers page structure, content hierarchy and the adaptation of materials to digital formats.

Content hierarchy

Headings, descriptions and images create different reading levels, from an overview to product details.

Photography and interface

I integrated the photographs and their editing into the website, keeping framing, backgrounds and the identity palette consistent.

05 / PRODUCT IMAGE

FORM, GRIP,
DETAIL.

I shot all the product photography and handled selection, editing and the final image treatment. General views show the controller, while close-ups document the grip, rear inputs, triggers and finishes.

06 / SOCIAL & CAMPAIGNS

MH4:
ONE COMPONENT PER POST.

I designed Instagram publications to present models, components and updates. The MH4 campaign uses a sequence of subjects: controller, rear buttons, Smart inputs, sticks and USB-C connectivity. Headlines, colour and product details hold the series together.

07 / PLAYER & TWITCH

THE BRAND

Player graphics bring Axion into Twitch channels: a static affiliate visual and an animated banner. New-colour announcements apply the same visual language to the vertical story format.

09 / EXPERIMENTAL LETTERING

LETTERS BUILT
FROM CONTROLLER INPUTS.

Experimental lettering derives the five letters of AXION from controller input shapes. The original composition becomes a display font with a dedicated interactive specimen.

Try the Axion lettering
