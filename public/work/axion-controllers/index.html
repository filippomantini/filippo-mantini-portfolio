AXIONCONTROLLERS

A visual identity, website and digital product system developed for a custom gaming controller brand, connecting product, performance, ecommerce and online configuration.

TAKE OVER. TAKE CONTROL. TAKE AXION.
01 / CONTEXT

A PERFORMANCE
FIRST BRAND.

Axion operates in a product category where technical performance and personal configuration are central to the customer experience.

The identity was developed to feel direct, technical and recognisable while remaining flexible enough for ecommerce, product launches and community communication.

02 / SYSTEM

IDENTITY MEETS
INTERFACE.

The brand extends across product presentation, a website designed around ecommerce, and a proprietary 3D controller configurator.

The digital experience is treated as part of the identity itself: product choices, visual hierarchy and interaction all contribute to how the brand is perceived.

03 / OUTPUT

FROM BRAND
TO BUILDER.

The project connects visual identity with a functional purchase experience, allowing customers to configure products rather than only browse them.

The system is designed to support an expanding product catalogue while keeping the brand language consistent.
