COLLAPSE

Collapse is an independent fashion project born in Umbria in 2023. More than a single identity or collection, it has grown into a connected creative world spanning garments, image-making, digital design, events and collaborations.

01 / IDENTITY & ART DIRECTION

OFF THE
GRID.

The original identity is built around the tension between structure and disruption. The Collapse logotype begins from a grid and deliberately moves outside it, turning the construction system itself into part of the visual language.

Over time the identity expands beyond a fixed brand manual: typography, graphics, labels, printed matter and campaign systems change with each context while keeping the project recognisable.

06 / COLLABORATIONS

A BRAND THAT
MEETS OTHERS.

Collaborations allow Collapse to temporarily absorb other visual languages without losing its own. Fashion, music, spaces, artists and external projects become opportunities to build limited identities, products and communication systems.

Only collaborations with a clear creative contribution will live here: what was designed, directed or produced matters more than the name attached to it.
