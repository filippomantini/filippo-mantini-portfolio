01 / ORIGIN & VISION

BUILDING
THE WORLD.

COLLAPSE was conceived in Città di Castello in 2021 by Filippo Mantini and Vittorio Volpi. My role runs through the building of the brand, from strategy and visual identity to the creative direction of collections and campaigns.

I did not join an already established project just to design a few assets. I helped shape its visual language and bring it into the real world through garments, imagery, communication, online retail, collaborations and events.

03 / FASHION & ART DIRECTION

FROM AN IDEA
TO AN IMAGE.

Art direction brings consistency to the brand: developing concepts, moodboards and styling, organising sets and collaborators, shooting, editorial content, campaigns and editing. Every shoot contributes to a recognisable language rather than existing as a one-off post.

The same thinking continues into product: shaping garments and their presentation, coordinating with suppliers, working on details and production. The creative and practical sides are part of one vision.
