01 / CONTEXT

COLLAPSE was conceived in Città di Castello in 2021 by Filippo Mantini and Vittorio Volpi. A shared vision where fashion, culture and identity develop together. From the beginning, my role has combined co-founding the project with creative direction.

COLLAPSE / CREATIVE DIRECTION
02 / THE STARTING POINT

PHILOSOPHY
BEFORE PRODUCT.

In its original brand book, COLLAPSE identifies two key principles: sustainability and fluidity. They are not decorative concepts, but ideas intended to guide garment research and the brand’s visual language.

The project’s stated vision focuses on more considered sourcing, material reuse, limited editions and an approach to clothing that challenges fixed gender categories.
