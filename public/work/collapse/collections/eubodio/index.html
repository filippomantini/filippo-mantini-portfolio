EUBODIO

The city leaves its mark.

From surface.
To garment.

Eubodio is a COLLAPSE capsule that brings attention to urban elements we pass without noticing: manhole covers, grilles, utility boxes and paving become printing matrices.

Water-based screen-printing ink was applied to selected surfaces and removed after use. Their traces were transferred onto fabric: direct contact between garment and city, resulting in 30 unique pieces.

The original postcards accompany the series with numbering from 1 to 30. Each garment is defined by its particular imprint, its irregularities and the placement of the print.
