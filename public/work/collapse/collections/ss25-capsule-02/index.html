01 / CAMPAIGN / SS25

ONE HOME.
A WHOLE
VISUAL WORLD.

Stairs, sofas, a bedroom and a wardrobe. The capsule enters an everyday setting, where clothes, people and objects become part of the same story.

02 / PHOTO STORY

LIVING IN
THE COLLECTION.

The photographs follow the garments through the home. An unmade bed, a game of chess, a moment beneath the staircase: the set becomes a place to wear the capsule.

03 / GRAPHIC IDENTITY

FREE
FLOWERS.

The flower becomes both a graphic sign and a physical object on set. The SS25 poster wraps the bouquets and reappears in the photographs, connecting the collection identity with its art direction.

04 / PRODUCT & STYLING

FROM GARMENT
TO DETAIL.

Raglan shirts, matching sets, denim and prints. Product studies and worn looks show the capsule’s materials, styling and graphic applications.

05 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

COLLAPSE
LOVERS.

Lettering and the launch composition extend the photographic story. Graphics, product and digital content share a recognisable visual language.

The capsule film and a moment from the set complete the photographic sequence. The documentation also shows the working space, lights and garments before the shoot.
