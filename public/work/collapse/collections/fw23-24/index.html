SELECTED COLLECTION

Fe26

Denim, manual treatments and industrial references. Fe26 explores garments and surfaces through product, photography and motion.

ROLECo-Founder & Creative Director
YEAR2023
PRACTICEFashion / Art Direction / Digital
01 / Fe26 / COLLECTION ARCHIVE

DENIM
AS A SURFACE.

Fe26 connects denim, surface treatments and industrial references. The project takes shape through garments, manual customisation and the imagery created to present them.

02 / MATERIAL & TREATMENT

MARKS.
SURFACES.
TREATMENTS.

The documentation brings together fronts, backs and details: warm marks on dark denim, light motifs and red applications on striped fabric. The images show the differences between the collection’s garments.

03 / GARMENTS & ACCESSORIES

THE PROCESS
BECOMES A GARMENT.

Silhouettes, details and accessories extend the surface research. Garments are shown on and off the body, with attention to volume, hems, chains and construction.

04 / ORIGINAL COMMUNICATION

THE VISUAL
WORLD OF Fe26.

The original imagery connects the product with industrial surfaces, sky and working vehicles. The archival material carries the Fe26 name and the SS23 reference.

05 / MOTION STUDIES

THE CONTEXT
IN MOTION.

Four short video compositions develop the project’s visual references: working vehicles, structures, metal surfaces and marks. Their original square format is preserved.

06 / MY CONTRIBUTION

CONCEPT
TO EXECUTION.

I worked on the identity, garment cutting and manual customisation, creative direction, shooting, editing and online communication.
