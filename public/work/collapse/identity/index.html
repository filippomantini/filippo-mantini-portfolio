01 / CONTEXT

The COLLAPSE logotype emerged from a design process combining a grid with typography. The original brand book documents construction, rotation, symbol synthesis, clear space and minimum size.

02 / CONSTRUCTION / PROCESS

GRID. TYPE.
ROTATION.

The brandmark originates from two elements: a stylised grid and the typographic component. In the documented process, the text is rotated by 5° to form the final logotype.

Identity does not end at the logo. The guidelines cover positive and negative versions, symbol simplification, clear space, placement and print requirements.

03 / CLEAR SPACE

PROTECTIVE SPACE

The original brand book defines a clear-space system measured in relation to the letter C.

04 / APPLICATION

SIZE & POSITION

The guidelines specify a 30 mm minimum width and prefer top-left placement for the symbol.

IDENTITY AS A SYSTEM

Consistency does not require repeating the same graphic everywhere. It comes from a small number of principles that allow the language to evolve across different formats.
