BUILDING
VISUAL WORLDS.

I work across identity, image, digital experiences and moving image, building systems that can live across different formats instead of stopping at a single output.

Trained in Graphic Design at NID Perugia, my practice moves between commercial work and experimental projects. I am interested in the point where graphic design stops being only a surface and becomes an experience: a garment, an interface, an exhibition, a photograph, a moving image or a physical object.

I like projects that need more than one language. The process often starts messy: references, tests, mistakes and fragments. The work is finding the relationships between them and turning that material into something clear enough to use, but open enough to keep evolving.
