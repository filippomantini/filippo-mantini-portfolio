Who manages this website

This website, filippomantini.com, is managed by Filippo Mantini. For questions concerning privacy or this website, you can contact info@filippomantini.com.

Data processed when you visit

The website is a static portfolio and does not provide user accounts, newsletter subscriptions or contact forms. Technical information necessary to deliver and secure the website may nevertheless be processed automatically when you visit, including network and request information such as IP addresses, timestamps and requested resources.

Hosting and infrastructure

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Cloudflare for domain, network and performance services. These providers may process technical data necessary to deliver, protect and operate the website in accordance with their respective privacy terms.

Website analytics

The website may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and website performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed to operate without cookies or local storage and without tracking individual visitors across websites. The information is used to understand page usage and improve the performance and usability of the portfolio.

External links

The website contains links to third-party services such as Behance and Instagram. Visiting those websites is subject to the privacy practices and terms of the respective providers. No social-media content is embedded solely by displaying these links.

Cookies

This portfolio does not intentionally use advertising or profiling cookies. If the technical configuration of the infrastructure changes or services requiring consent are introduced, this notice and the website's consent mechanisms will be updated accordingly.

Your rights and contact

Where applicable under data-protection law, you may request information about personal data concerning you, and request its correction, deletion, restriction or exercise other rights provided by law. Requests concerning this website can be sent to info@filippomantini.com.
